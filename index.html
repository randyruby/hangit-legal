Hang-It Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Hang-It ("the app") is made by RJR Software LLC. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Short version

Hang-It does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter — wall measurements, frame sizes, saved walls, and your unit preference — is stored only on your device. We never see it.

What the app stores

The app saves your walls, frames, and settings locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data is never sent to RJR Software LLC or any third party, and there is no account or sign-in of any kind.

Camera

Hang-It does not use your camera.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

In-app purchases

If you purchase a Pro upgrade, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple's or Google's app store — RJR Software LLC does not receive or process your payment information.

Children's privacy

Hang-It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: rjrsoftwarellc@gmail.com
