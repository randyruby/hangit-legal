Hang-It Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Hang-It ("the app") is made by RJR Software LLC. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Short version

Hang-It does not collect your walls, frame sizes, or any content you enter. Everything you enter — wall measurements, frame sizes, saved walls, and your unit preference — is stored only on your device. We never see it. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase check described under "In-app purchases" below.

What the app stores

The app saves your walls, frames, and settings locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data is never sent to RJR Software LLC or any third party, and there is no account or sign-in of any kind.

Backups

The backup feature creates a file on your device that you choose where to save or share. RJR Software LLC does not receive it.

Camera

Hang-It does not use your camera.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. It does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Hang-It has a one-time Pro upgrade. The payment itself is handled entirely by Apple's or Google's app store — RJR Software LLC does not receive or process your payment information.

To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy), a service that checks purchases. When the app opens, and when you buy or restore Pro, it sends RevenueCat a randomly generated anonymous ID and your purchase receipt from the store, and RevenueCat replies whether Pro is unlocked. As with any internet request, RevenueCat also receives your IP address and basic technical details such as the app version, device type, operating system, language, and currency. This is not linked to your name, email address, or any account, because Hang-It has no accounts, and it is used only to provide the Pro purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

To have this information removed, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Hang-It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only data it sends is the anonymous purchase check described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
